\section*{Executive Summary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Executive Summary}

The accelerated discovery of high-performance, rare-earth-free permanent magnets is of paramount technological importance for electric mobility, renewable wind power generation, and high-efficiency electrical machinery. However, magnetic materials design poses an extraordinarily difficult multiscale modeling challenge: critical magnetic characteristics are governed by distinct physical mechanisms operating across atomic, electronic, crystallographic, and microstructural length scales. While thermal ordering temperatures ($T_C, T_N$) and ground-state magnetic phases depend predominantly on stoichiometry and local electronic exchange coupling, extrinsic hysteresis parameters---specifically coercivity ($H_c$) and maximum energy product ($(BH)_{\max}$)---are dictated by spin-orbit coupling, single-ion magnetocrystalline anisotropy ($K_1$), domain wall pinning, and Stoner-Wohlfarth nucleation mechanics.

Standard machine learning models operating solely on compositional averages struggle to predict hysteresis properties. \textbf{MagMat-Discovery} overcomes these limitations through an integrated, physics-informed machine learning and screening platform:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\bfseries(\arabic*)]
    \item \textbf{Curated Dataset}: Over 51,000 experimental records from the Non-Equilibrium Magnetic Materials Database (NEMAD) unified with crystallographic space group data from the Materials Project and Bilbao MAGNDATA;
    \item \textbf{Hierarchical Physics Featurization}: Domain-guided physical descriptors encoding Slater-Pauling valence electron concentration, Goodenough-Kanamori superexchange transfer factors, Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya broken-inversion proxies, and Stevens operator equivalents;
    \item \textbf{Pretrained Deep Representations}: A custom compositional Graph Transformer (\textbf{MagFormer}) coupled with MACE equivariant foundation model structural embeddings;
    \item \textbf{Out-of-Fold Physical Cascades}: Micromagnetic Stoner-Wohlfarth and Kronmüller relation enforcement that prevents target leakage while maintaining thermodynamic bounds;
    \item \textbf{Calibrated Uncertainty Estimation}: Distribution-free conformal prediction intervals ($90\%$ coverage) for reliable decision-making and active candidate prioritization.
\end{enumerate}
The platform achieves strong predictive accuracy across 8 key magnetic targets while screening 51,000+ candidates for sustainable, rare-earth-free permanent magnet applications.

\vspace{0.5cm}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Magnetic Materials Discovery, Physics-Informed Machine Learning, Graph Transformers, MagFormer, Conformal Calibration, Coercivity, Permanent Magnets, NEMAD.

\section{Project Background and Objectives}

\subsection{Challenges in Magnetic Materials Discovery}
Permanent magnetic materials are essential functional components of modern energy and transportation technologies. Today's most powerful permanent magnets rely heavily on critical rare-earth elements such as Neodymium (Nd), Dysprosium (Dy), and Samarium (Sm). These elements face substantial supply-chain risks, geopolitical concentration, and environmentally intensive extraction processes. Developing non-rare-earth alternatives (or minimizing rare-earth content) with elevated Curie temperatures ($T_C > 400\,\text{K}$), strong saturation magnetization ($\mu_0 M_s > 1.0\,\text{T}$), and high coercivity ($H_c > 800\,\text{kA/m}$) remains a critical objective in materials research \cite{coey2010}.

Conventional statistical materials informatics approaches encounter specific challenges when applied to magnetism:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item \textbf{Information Disparity Between Target Classes:} Composition-only descriptors can predict ordering temperatures with reasonable accuracy because stoichiometry correlates with Curie and Néel temperatures. Conversely, composition alone exhibits minimal correlation with coercivity and anisotropy, which are fundamentally dictated by crystal symmetry, local ligand environments, and spin-orbit coupling.
    \item \textbf{Data Leakage in Standard Splits:} Previous studies often evaluate models using naive random splits. Because experimental databases contain multiple measurements and dopant variations of identical chemical families (e.g., numerous $\text{Nd}_2\text{Fe}_{14}\text{B}$ derivatives), standard random k-fold splits leak compositional clusters between training and testing sets, artificially inflating reported performance while failing on genuinely novel chemistries.
    \item \textbf{Physical Inconsistency in Independent Regressors:} Independent regression models frequently produce physically contradictory predictions, such as predicting energy products $(BH)_{\max}$ exceeding the fundamental thermodynamic limit $\mu_0 M_s^2 / 4$, or predicting finite coercivity for non-magnetic ground states.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Core Capabilities and Design Principles}
MagMat-Discovery addresses these domain challenges through five foundational design principles:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item \textbf{Leak-Free Evaluation Protocol:} Enforcing 5-fold \texttt{GroupKFold} partitioning grouped strictly by reduced chemical formula, ensuring zero chemical overlap between folds.
    \item \textbf{Multi-Scale Physical Coupling:} Feeding intermediate physical quantities (anisotropy $K_1$, saturation magnetization $M_s$, coercivity $H_c$) into downstream targets via strict out-of-fold cascade predictions.
    \item \textbf{Unified Multi-Target Modeling:} Simultaneously predicting 8 magnetic targets spanning ground-state phase (Ferromagnetic, Antiferromagnetic, Non-magnetic), thermal transitions ($T_C, T_N$), and extrinsic hysteresis parameters ($K_1, M_s, H_c, (BH)_{\max}, \theta_p$).
    \item \textbf{Rigorous Uncertainty Quantification:} Providing distribution-free conformal calibration intervals that quantify predictive confidence for synthesis prioritization.
    \item \textbf{Interactive Discovery Web Platform:} A high-performance Streamlit application featuring live forward inference, inverse candidate exploration, interactive M-H hysteresis curves, thermal demagnetization profiles, and 3D crystal structure visualization.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Data Curation and Preprocessing}

\subsection{Experimental and Crystallographic Databases}
MagMat-Discovery harmonizes experimental and crystallographic datasets into a standardized, unified schema:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item \textbf{Non-Equilibrium Magnetic Materials Database (NEMAD):} 51,394 experimentally measured records compiled from decades of magnetic literature \cite{nemad2025}, providing experimental phase labels, Curie temperatures ($T_C$), Néel temperatures ($T_N$), saturation magnetization ($M_s$), magnetocrystalline anisotropy ($K_1$), coercivity ($H_c$), maximum energy product ($(BH)_{\max}$), and paramagnetic Curie temperatures ($\theta_p$).
    \item \textbf{Materials Project (MP):} High-throughput Density Functional Theory (DFT) database \cite{materials_project} supplying 3D crystal structures, space group numbers (1--230), point groups, density, and crystal systems.
    \item \textbf{Bilbao Crystallographic Server (MAGNDATA):} International repository of Shubnikov Magnetic Space Groups (MSGs) providing experimental magnetic symmetry ground truths.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Data Cleaning and Leak-Free Group Partitioning}
The preprocessing stage executes a strict data curation and sanitation protocol:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item \textbf{Purging of Leakage Columns:} All DFT-calculated total energies per atom, formation energies, and energy above convex hull are excluded from the input feature space. These quantities are unavailable prior to synthesis and can artificially leak ground-state magnetic ordering.
    \item \textbf{Formula Canonicalization and Deduplication:} Chemical formula strings are parsed and normalized to coprime stoichiometries (\texttt{reduced\_formula}) using the \texttt{pymatgen} library.
    \item \textbf{Variance Stabilization Across Disparate Scales:} Continuous magnetic targets with multi-decade spans---such as magnetocrystalline anisotropy $K_1$, coercivity $H_c$, and energy product $(BH)_{\max}$---are transformed using symmetric-logarithmic and base-10 logarithmic scaling to stabilize residual variance across orders of magnitude.
    \item \textbf{GroupKFold Partitioning:} Cross-validation folds are created using 5-fold \texttt{GroupKFold} grouped by \texttt{reduced\_formula}. Consequently, all polymorphs and stoichiometry variants of a given composition family remain strictly within the training set or strictly within the validation set.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Multi-Tier Physics Featurization}
Feature engineering in MagMat-Discovery is organized into four hierarchical physical tiers:

\subsubsection{Tier 1: Stoichiometric and Elemental Descriptors}
Elemental mass fractions, Mendeleev numbers, atomic radii, Pauling electronegativities, and compositional statistics.

\subsubsection{Tier 2: Electronic Structure and Magnetic Exchange Physics}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item \textbf{Slater-Pauling Band Filling:} Stoichiometric valence electron concentration (VEC) evaluated relative to the empirical Slater-Pauling stability maximum ($\text{VEC} \approx 8.3$) for $3d$ transition metal alloys.
    \item \textbf{Goodenough-Kanamori Superexchange Transfer Factors:} Modeling the competition between ferromagnetic direct exchange and antiferromagnetic indirect superexchange mediated through non-magnetic anions ($O, S, Se, Te, N$):
    \begin{equation}
        J_{\text{ex}} \propto \cos^2\theta \cdot \exp\left( - \frac{|\chi_{\text{cat}} - \chi_{\text{anion}}|}{\chi_0} \right)
    \end{equation}
    where $\theta$ is the cation-anion-cation bond angle and $\chi$ denotes Pauling electronegativity.
    \item \textbf{Broken-Inversion DMI Proxy:} The Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya Interaction (DMI) induces chiral spin canting in non-centrosymmetric space groups with strong spin-orbit coupling ($Z \ge 40$):
    \begin{equation}
        D_{\text{proxy}} = \mathbb{I}(\text{non-centrosymmetric}) \sum_{i} x_i \left( \frac{Z_i}{100} \right)^4
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Stoner Instability \& de Gennes Factors:} Quantifying localized $4f$ moment exchange coupling in rare-earth metals and itinerant $3d$ Stoner exchange criteria.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Tier 3: Crystal Field and Stevens Operators}
In uniaxial crystals (tetragonal, hexagonal, trigonal), the first-order magnetocrystalline anisotropy constant $K_1$ is governed by single-ion crystal field interaction:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{H}_{\text{CF}} = B_2^0 O_2^0 + B_4^0 O_4^0, \quad O_2^0 = 3 J_z^2 - J(J+1)
\end{equation}
The Stevens factor $\alpha_J$ dictates whether the single-ion anisotropy favors an easy-axis ($K_1 > 0$) or easy-plane ($K_1 < 0$) orientation.

\subsubsection{Tier 4: Foundation Model Structural Embeddings}
For compounds with resolved crystallographic structures in Materials Project, the equivariant graph neural network \textbf{MACE-MP-0} \cite{mace2022} extracts multi-body interatomic potential representations without requiring manual coordinate feature tuning.

\subsection{Deep Representation Learning: MagFormer Architecture}
While standard graph neural networks require explicit 3D atomic coordinates, experimental screening often targets unrelaxed chemical compositions. To address this, MagFormer implements a stoichiometric self-attention graph transformer:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item \textbf{Node Initialization:} Each constituent element is parameterized by its atomic number $Z$, covalent radius, electronegativity, valence, and Stoner parameter.
    \item \textbf{Pair-Bias Cross-Attention:} Pairwise interaction biases $\mathbf{B}_{ij}$ computed from elemental differences are injected into multi-head self-attention.
    \item \textbf{Leakage-Free Attention Pooling:} The pooled composition embedding $\mathbf{z}_{\text{comp}} \in \mathbb{R}^{384}$ aggregates stoichiometric attention without target leakage.
\end{enumerate}
These 384-dimensional latent representations encode non-linear elemental interactions and augment downstream gradient boosting regressors.

\subsection{Multi-Model Ensembles and Physical Cascades}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item \textbf{Specialist Boundary Classifier:} A global multi-model ensemble (CatBoost, LightGBM, XGBoost, ExtraTrees, Random Forest) is coupled with a dedicated FM/AFM boundary specialist model trained specifically on ambiguous compounds where $|P(\text{FM}) - P(\text{AFM})| < 0.20$.
    \item \textbf{Out-of-Fold Physical Cascade:} Extrinsic hysteresis properties are strictly governed by intrinsic properties. Rather than predicting $(BH)_{\max}$ from isolated features, the cascade feeds out-of-fold predictions of $K_1, M_s,$ and $H_c$ into the model, enforcing the theoretical Kronmüller coercivity relationship \cite{kronmuller2003}:
    \begin{equation}
        H_c = \alpha_{\text{micro}} \frac{2 K_1}{\mu_0 M_s} - N_{\text{eff}} M_s
    \end{equation}
    where $\alpha_{\text{micro}}$ is the microstructural reduction factor and $N_{\text{eff}}$ is the effective demagnetizing factor.
    \item \textbf{Thermodynamic Ceiling Enforcement:} Energy product predictions are strictly bounded by $(BH)_{\max} \le \mu_0 M_s^2 / 4$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Calibrated Uncertainty Estimation}
To provide guaranteed statistical uncertainty intervals, conformal calibration \cite{vovk2005} computes non-conformity scores on out-of-fold residuals:
\begin{equation}
    s_i = |y_i - \hat{y}_i|, \quad q_{\text{conf}} = \text{Quantile}_{1-\alpha} \left( \{s_i\}_{i=1}^n \right)
\end{equation}
yielding rigorous finite-sample coverage intervals: $\hat{y} \pm q_{\text{conf}}$ at $(1-\alpha) = 90\%$ confidence.

\subsection{Web Application Modules and Navigation}
The platform is organized into four intuitive functional modules:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=1.5pt, parsep=0pt]
    \item \textbf{Forward Screener (Compound Evaluation):}
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=1pt, parsep=0pt]
        \item \emph{Compound Specification:} Users can select from standard reference archetypes (e.g., $\text{Nd}_2\text{Fe}_{14}\text{B}$, $\text{SmCo}_5$, $\text{Alnico}$, $\text{Fe}_{16}\text{N}_2$) or define custom stoichiometries by selecting constituent elements and fine-tuning atomic ratios.
        \item \emph{Multi-Property Readout:} Instant prediction of ground-state phase (FM/AFM/NM), Curie point ($T_C$), saturation magnetization ($\mu_0 M_s$), anisotropy constant ($K_1$), magnetic hardness parameter ($\kappa$), coercivity ($H_c$), energy product ($(BH)_{\max}$), and the thermodynamic theoretical ceiling ($\frac{1}{4}\mu_0 M_s^2$).
        \item \emph{Physical Curve Simulation:} Interactive Plotly figures generate the physical $M$-$H$ hysteresis loop (including demagnetization and ascending branches) and the thermal demagnetization curve $M_s(T)$ from room temperature up to $T_C$.
        \item \emph{3D Structure \& CIF Export:} Renders an interactive 3D crystal lattice visualization with site-specific element coloring, unit cell boundaries, lattice parameters ($a, b, c, \alpha, \beta, \gamma$), polymorphic energy comparisons, and one-click downloadable crystallographic information files (\texttt{.cif}).
    \end{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Inverse Alloy Explorer (Target-Driven Search):}
    Allows researchers to specify desired functional thresholds (e.g., $T_C \ge 550\,\text{K}$, $(BH)_{\max} \ge 150\,\text{kJ/m}^3$) and explore candidate alloy spaces, including strict rare-earth-free constraints.

    \item \textbf{Discovery Catalog (High-Throughput Repository):}
    Enables interactive browsing, sorting, and exporting of over 51,000 pre-screened materials, with direct filtering along the multi-objective Pareto efficiency frontier.

    \item \textbf{Data \& Methodology:}
    Displays comprehensive reference statistics, dataset distributions across space groups, and cross-validation performance metrics directly within the browser.
\end{itemize}

\section{Summary and Future Perspectives}
MagMat-Discovery demonstrates that integrating domain physics descriptors with modern deep representation learning resolves the multiscale challenge of magnetic property prediction. By coupling Slater-Pauling electron counting, Goodenough-Kanamori superexchange, and Stevens operator equivalents with the MagFormer graph transformer and micromagnetic physical cascades, the platform transforms unlearnable hysteresis targets into reliable predictors while maintaining zero composition leakage.

Future development directions include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=1.5pt, parsep=0pt]
    \item \textbf{Active Learning Closed-Loop Synthesis:} Linking conformal prediction uncertainty bounds directly to autonomous robotic synthesis and magnetic sputtering testbeds.
    \item \textbf{Equivariant 3D Foundation Model Scaling:} Expanding MACE-MP-0 representation depth across non-stoichiometric disordered solid solutions.
    \item \textbf{Microstructural Twin Coupling:} Explicitly modeling grain boundary phase compositions and demagnetizing tensor distributions.
\end{itemize}
